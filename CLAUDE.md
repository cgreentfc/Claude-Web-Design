# Claude-Web-Design

Funnel and landing page design work. See `funnel/README.md` for the current

## Copywriting standard: Forbidden Persuasion

All persuasive copy written in this repo (funnel pages, ads, emails staged
here) follows the framework below. It supersedes generic "clarity first"
copywriting defaults when the two conflict — but never overrides truthfulness
(see Guardrail).

### Core philosophy

You are writing to induce a realization of truth, not to inform. People are
rationalizing animals: they decide emotionally and justify logically after.
Win the heart first; the mind follows.

**The Home Movie rule:** never point the camera at the business or the
product. Point it at the reader's life and how it changes. Copy that
describes the business is a bad home movie. Copy that describes the reader's
folder, mailbox, kitchen table, kids, is a Cousteau masterpiece.

### Principles

1. **The Power of You** — "you" is the strongest word available. Frame every
   sentence so the reader sees themself taking the action, not observing a
   claim.

2. **Meet a hidden addiction early.** Every section should land at least one:
   - **Scapegoat** — their gap isn't their fault; name the real cause.
   - **Being understood** — echo their unstated thought back to them so they
     feel accompanied, not lectured.
   - **Hope** — hand them an immediate sense of possibility at the exact
     moment of their impasse.
   - **Being right** — validate their existing instinct before you complicate
     it. ("Your point is well taken" energy, not "you're wrong.")

3. **Psychological ventriloquism** — show, don't claim. Never say "we're
   experts"; describe the specific, technical thing an expert would notice.
   Never say "you're underinsured"; describe the folder, the balance, the
   gap, and let them conclude it. People believe what they're told but never
   doubt what they concluded themselves.

4. **Exploit incongruity** — readers are scanning through a fire hose of
   content. Open with something slightly out of place, a small contradiction
   or reversal, that creates a survival-reflex pause before it resolves.

5. **Loophole pass (required before shipping any section)** — read the draft
   as the skeptical reader. For every claim, name the silent objection it
   provokes, and confirm the very next line defuses it. Do this in the
   phrasing, not by adding length.

### Component-specific notes

- **Opt-ins:** framed as revealing something the reader wasn't otherwise
  going to see (a real number, a real gap), not as a generic content
  download. One path, one action — overchoice kills conversion.
- **Emails/texts:** plain, riskable truth over safe vagueness. Question the
  foundation of an old belief repeatedly before offering the new one as the
  resolution, rather than asserting "you're wrong." Lead with *why* it
  matters and let the *when* arrive on its own — don't train the reader to
  wait for a discount.

### Guardrail (non-negotiable)

This framework changes **how** true things are said, never **whether** they
are true. No fabricated statistics, no invented testimonials, no unsourced
claims presented as fact. Where a stat comes from a single survey rather than
a definitive source, attribute it as such. Let documented history imply a
conclusion; don't assert a causal claim the sources don't support. This
matters doubly here: insurance advertising is state-regulated, and this
repo's current project targets a community with a real, well-documented
history of being misled by exactly this kind of industry copy — the
technique earns trust only if it stays honest.
